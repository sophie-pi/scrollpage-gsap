Welcome to the Borderland

In the blink of an eye, Tokyo becomes a desolate wasteland. Arisu and his friends find themselves trapped in a parallel world where survival depends on winning twisted games.

Each game is a deadly puzzle marked by playing cards, where the suit determines the type of challenge and the number indicates its difficulty.

The rules are simple: play the game, clear it, and earn visa extensions. Fail, and face execution by the mysterious lasers from the sky.

But who created this world? What lies beyond the games? And most importantly, how do you escape the Borderland?

The Players

01

Ryōhei Arisu

The Strategist

An intelligent but unmotivated gamer who finds purpose in the Borderland. His analytical mind and strategic thinking make him a natural at solving deadly puzzles. Despite his skills, his compassion for others often puts him at risk, forcing him to balance survival with his humanity.

02

Yuzuha Usagi

The Survivor

A fierce and athletic climber who survived the Borderland alone before meeting Arisu. Her physical prowess and survival instincts are matched only by her determination to honor her father's memory. She becomes Arisu's anchor in this brutal world.

03

Shuntarō Chishiya

The Manipulator

A mysterious and calculating strategist who trusts no one. His cold, logical approach to the games makes him incredibly dangerous. Behind his enigmatic smile lies a brilliant mind that's always three steps ahead, though his true motives remain hidden.

04

Suguru Niragi

The Monster

A violent militant who fully embraces the chaos of the Borderland. Once a victim of relentless bullying, he now wields his rifle and sadistic nature to instill fear in others. The Borderland has unleashed the darkest parts of his soul.

05

Mira Kano

The Architect

Executive of The Beach and later revealed as a game master. Her cheerful, almost playful demeanor masks a deeply disturbing psychological complexity. She understands the human psyche better than anyone, making her the most dangerous player of all.

The Deadly Games

Every card suit represents a different challenge: ♠ Physical, ♥ Psychological, ♣ Teamwork, and ♦ Intelligence. The number shows the difficulty. Face cards are the final trials.
♠

Five of Spades

Hide and Seek - A deadly game of tag set in a massive apartment complex. Players must evade a superhuman tagger whose touch means instant death. The only escape is finding the safe zone before time runs out, but the tagger learns and adapts with each encounter.

5♠ Physical
♥

Seven of Hearts

Witch Hunt - A psychological nightmare where players must identify the witch among them. Trust crumbles as everyone becomes a suspect. Make the wrong choice and everyone dies. Choose correctly and watch an innocent person burn. There are no winners in games of hearts.

7♥ Psychological
♦

King of Diamonds

Beauty Contest - The ultimate test of logic and game theory. Players must predict the average choice of others in an intricate mathematical puzzle. Only those who can think several moves ahead and understand human nature will survive this battle of wits.

K♦ Intelligence
♣

King of Clubs

Osmosis - A game of perfect teamwork and sacrifice. Players must work together to solve increasingly impossible challenges, but the game is designed to turn allies into enemies. When cooperation means certain death for some, who will make the ultimate sacrifice?

K♣ Teamwork
♠

King of Spades

The King of Spades is not a venue but a person - a relentless mercenary who hunts players across the entire Borderland. Armed with military-grade weapons and tactical genius, he never stops, never sleeps. Run, hide, or fight - nothing can stop the King of Spades.

K♠ Survival
♥

Queen of Hearts

Croquet - The final game. Mira's psychological labyrinth where reality itself becomes the puzzle. Players must confront their deepest truths and decide what reality truly means. In a world built on illusions, the hardest game is accepting the truth about yourself.

Q♥ Mind Break
